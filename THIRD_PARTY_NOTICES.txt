AI Lab Helper includes a Python runtime and Tcl/Tk packaged by PyInstaller.
Python license: https://docs.python.org/3/license.html
PyInstaller license and bootloader exception: https://pyinstaller.org/en/stable/license.html
Tcl/Tk license: https://www.tcl-lang.org/software/tcltk/license.html

Arduino CLI and AVR tools are not bundled in this installer. On the user's request,
the application downloads them from the official Arduino distribution services.
Arduino CLI v0.35.3 release and source: https://github.com/arduino/arduino-cli/releases/tag/v0.35.3
Arduino AVR core: https://github.com/arduino/ArduinoCore-avr/tree/1.8.6
Optional libraries are downloaded by Arduino CLI only when requested by the user.

Optional CH340 recovery downloads the unmodified WCH 3.7.2022.1 Windows driver files only when needed. Copyright W.ch 2001-2022. Original Microsoft-signed catalog and driver binaries are preserved. Source: Arduino Help Center article 13148652511260 and its archived WCH CH341SER.EXE link. No WCH driver is bundled in this installer.
